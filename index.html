I · The Shadow of 1734

India kept its own time in 1734.

In Jaipur, Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II built the Vrihat Samrat Yantra, a 27 metre sundial that reads the sun to about two seconds. Drag the sun and let the shadow read the hour.

III · The Heart

Spin the heart.

A tourbillon turns the whole escapement once a minute so the pull of gravity averages out. Abraham-Louis Breguet patented the idea in 1801. On the Samrat it sits open at six o'clock and doubles as the seconds hand.
